What I do

Capabilities

How I can help.

Form development, CMF and physical models. Sketch through foam, 3D print and hand-finished prototypes, specified so the thing can actually be made.

Interfaces with real states, thresholds and edge cases, from first flow to a working front-end build. Dashboards, booking flows, connected-device apps.

Field studies, contextual interviews and synthesis that actually reach the design, not a deck that gets filed away. Most of my projects start here.

Visual systems that hold up past the logo: type, colour, motion and the rules that keep it consistent as it scales across surfaces.

My process

How it runs

Four steps, no mystery.

01

Discover

Understand the people, the constraints and what success actually looks like before anything gets drawn.

02

Frame

Turn the research into a sharp brief: the problem worth solving, and the edges around it.

03

Design

Flows, states and visual system. Reviewed against the brief, not against taste alone.

04

Build

Developed into something responsive and real, then tested, handed over and documented.
