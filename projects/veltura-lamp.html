Veltura Lamp

Paper plane study table lamp

Objective

To design and prototype a functional study table lamp in a modern interior style, running a 5W to 9W light source and built predominantly from paper and cardboard.

The process

  1. Mood and direction
  2. Ideation sketching
  3. Model making
  4. Light source

The brief

A functional study table lamp in a modern interior style, made predominantly from paper and cardboard, carrying a 5W to 9W source. The constraint was structural as much as visual: it had to stand up, balance and be handled.

Ideation sketching

Dumping the random ideas first, then narrowing towards the concept that carried the paper plane form: a folded wing, a single spar, and a tripod that reads as light as the shade it holds.

Model making

Skewer sticks for the frame, mount board for the wings, a cardboard tube for the stem and black chart paper to finish it. The frame was built first and proved against its own weight before the shade went on.

Light and structure

LED strips run along the inside edge of the wing, so the source is never seen directly. The light washes down the underside of the shade and reaches the desk as a soft, even field rather than a hot spot.

The finished lamp

Photographed outdoors and on the desk it was built for. The wing reads as one folded plane from the front and almost disappears edge-on, which is the whole point of the paper plane reference.
