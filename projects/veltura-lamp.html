Veltura Lamp

Paper plane study table lamp

Objective

To design and prototype a functional study table lamp in a modern interior style, running a 5W to 9W light source and built predominantly from paper and cardboard.

The process

  1. Mood and direction
  2. Ideation sketching
  3. Model making
  4. Light source

The brief

A study lamp in paper and cardboard for a 5W to 9W source. It had to stand, balance and be handled, not just look right.

Ideation sketching

Random ideas first, then the concept that carried the paper plane: a folded wing, a single spar and a light tripod.

Model making

Skewer frame, mount board wings, a cardboard tube stem and black chart paper, proved against its own weight first.

Light and structure

LED strips along the inside edge of the wing, so the source is never seen and the desk gets an even wash.

The finished lamp

One folded plane from the front, and almost nothing edge on.
