Identity for the IIT Roorkee convocation

The brief

An institution-wide identity that took three months: two moving through several themes and rounds of approval, and one putting it on campus. Every draft went through the administration and its committees, and the feedback was frequent, direct and often tough. The job was to keep one clear system intact through those rounds and still deliver every pass, banner, folder and stage piece on time.

Three months, start to stage

Two months on themes and approvals, one on execution.

A national stage

The institute’s most important day of the year: two ceremonies over 19–20 September, 2,701 degrees, and the National Security Advisor and the Chief of Defence Staff on stage. The identity had to hold its own beside them, and in every photograph taken that weekend.

Campus Bloom

The theme for 2026. The SDG flower carries the 17 Sustainable Development Goals as 17 coloured petals and takes the place of the O in the wordmark. Three flowers mark the three programmes, and the main building is drawn in the same leaf pattern.
