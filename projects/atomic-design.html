Object for inspiration

The F-22 Raptor and F-35: a futuristic language that already belongs to aviation.

why a fighter jet

Sharp, fast and precise, the way a site for aviation photographs should feel.

Moodboard

Angular, escape, stealthy, agility, metallic. Orange, graphite, navy and taupe carried into the palette.
