Product design · Lighting

BLICK

Desk lamp for IKEA

  • Solid pine throughout, cut and jointed so the construction stays visible.
  • A linear LED strip recessed into the arm, so the source never glares and the light lands even.
  • A planter set into the base, so the lamp holds a small plant as well as a light.
  • A single pivot at the head, letting the arm swing to aim the beam.
  • Cable routed along the arm and out through the base.
