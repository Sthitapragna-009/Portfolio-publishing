Form design · CMF · Product

AeroForm 009

Hand held vacuum cleaner

Objective

To design a modern hand held vacuum cleaner for Braun.

The process

  1. Setting the moodboard
  2. Form exploration
  3. CMF
  4. 3D printed model

Setting the moodboard

Braun’s own language, gathered first: aerodynamic plus minimalism, less is more, good design equals long lasting. Orange, graphite, bronze and off white came out of it, and carried straight through to the product.

Form exploration

Dozens of handheld silhouettes narrowed to one: a flat, wing like body cantilevered over an open loop handle. Then the internals: an exploded assembly and a set of orthographic views to fix the proportions.

Material selection

Three materials, each chosen for what it has to do: hold the form, show the dirt, and take the hand.

  • Body

    ABS plastic with a rubber texture

  • Grip

    Black textured rubberised grip in TPU

  • Dial

    Orange pop dial in rubberised ABS with ridges for grip, plus a haptic feedback scroll wheel for suction control

  • Collector

    Frosted glass finish dust collector in polycarbonate

Colour, material, finish

This design aims to create a handheld vacuum cleaner that is both functional and aesthetically pleasing, aligning with Braun’s design legacy and the minimalist style. One orange, used only where the hand goes.

Colour options

Eight finishes on the production form.

Design for repairability

Easy durable fits, repair over replace, and a DIY repair guide. The control pod lifts out on a ribbon connector rather than being bonded in.

3D printed model

Printed, finished and painted in two parts: a white body and a black handle, with the screen and the orange dial mocked up to check them at size and in the hand.

Intuitive UI and modes

Suction is a scroll wheel, not a row of buttons. Three states, each with its own force setting and colour: Custom on set force, Boost on full force, eco on low force.
